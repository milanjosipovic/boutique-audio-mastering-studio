How a master gets made

The signal chain

Five passes, in this order, every time. Nothing is skipped, and nothing happens twice — a mastering chain is a sequence, not a preset.

  1. 01

    Listen

    A full reference pass against the mix notes and the artist's own touchstones. No processing yet — just finding what the record is trying to be.

  2. 02

    Balance

    Tonal correction through the analog EQ — carving space in the low mids, restoring air up top, before anything digital touches the signal.

  3. 03

    Glue

    Gentle bus compression to knit the mix into one moving thing, tuned to the tempo and the transients already in the recording.

  4. 04

    Peak

    True-peak limiting to the loudness target for the intended format — streaming and vinyl are never limited to the same number.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Dithered stems, DDP for the pressing plant, WAV for distribution — checked back to back against the reference before anything ships.

What the signal passes through

The studio

Studio equipment list
Monitoring Nearfield & midfield pair, matched for phase, room-corrected
Preamp Class-A tube preamp, used for warmth on the input stage
Equalizer Passive program EQ + digital surgical EQ
Compression Vari-mu bus compressor, digital multiband as needed
Limiting True-peak limiter, format-aware ceiling per delivery target
Conversion Mastering-grade A/D–D/A, clocked and calibrated quarterly
Delivery DDP 2.0, 16/24-bit WAV, dithered stems on request
